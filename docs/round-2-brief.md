# AllesGute round 2: who it's for, what she gets, what to shoot

Buyer: a busy, posh millennial mother in Berlin or Märkisch-Oderland. She wants her child's party to look beautiful and be done for her, with food she's happy her kids eat.

## Brands she already trusts

These come from general knowledge of the brands. Their sites were not re-checked for this brief.

| Brand | What it teaches |
|---|---|
| Bonpoint (Paris) | Cream ground, a refined serif, huge calm photos, almost no copy |
| Konges Sløjd (DK) | Muted oat, sage and blush, nostalgic and soft, never shouty |
| Liewood (DK) | Rounded shapes, sage and butter fields, friendly sans, tidy grids |
| Maileg (DK) | Vintage warmth and tiny details, linen and cream |
| Meri Meri (UK) | Party goods styled on white, colour comes from the product, not the page |
| Jacadi (Paris) | Classic and polished, short product names, one accent colour |
| Ottolenghi | Food piled high and bright: healthy reads as abundant, not as a diet |
| Styled party studios (grazing-table and balloon stylists on Instagram) | One hero table shot in daylight, sold as named packages with an "ab" price |

What they share:
- An airy cream or oat ground.
- One big, bright, styled photo per screen.
- Muted sage, blush and butter, with a single strong accent.
- A serif or a soft geometric face, and short copy.
- Children shown as hands and backs, not posed faces.
- Few choices, each one named.

## The offering

Proposed prices. Paulina confirms them.

| Package | ab | She gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Der Tisch** | 150 € | A styled, healthy-and-beautiful table: fruit, vegetables, waffles, cake, with labels. Paulina sets it up and clears it away. |
| **Die Feier** | 320 € | The table, plus a cake and room decoration in the child's favourite theme, plus one baking or play round that Paulina leads. |
| **Ihr seid Gäste** | 490 € | Everything above, plus take-home bags and tidying up to the last plate. The parents arrive with their child like every other guest. |

What she no longer has to do: shopping, baking, buying decorations, setting the table, keeping the children busy, washing up.

## Styled shoot: 10 shots (Paulina's own; never stock, never AI)

Shoot in daylight by a window. Use a linen or white cloth, glass and ceramic plates, no paper plates, and no licensed characters.

1. Paulina's face, half-smiling, holding a finished cake. Portrait format.
2. One full styled table from the front, shot at child's eye height, with nobody in the frame.
3. The same table from above (flat lay).
4. Children's hands reaching for berries. No faces.
5. Close-up of a handwritten label next to its dish.
6. The naked cake with berries, alone on a cake stand against a plain wall.
7. A bowl of cut vegetables with dip, colourful and bright: the "healthy" shot.
8. Paulina's hands arranging fruit (the work, done for her).
9. A take-home bag or box, tied, on the cloth.
10. The cleared, clean room afterwards: the "you didn't wash up" shot.
